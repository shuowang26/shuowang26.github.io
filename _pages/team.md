Welcome to the AIM³ (AI for Multi-Modal Medicine) research group at Fudan University, based on the medical campus. We are an interdisciplinary team dedicated to addressing clinical challenges from affiliated hospitals and exploring the fundamental scientific principles of health and disease.

Our members bring expertise in mathematics, mechanics, physics, computer science, electrical engineering, biomedical engineering, basic medicine, and clinical medicine. Together, we strive to accelerate medical discovery and improve patient care through innovative AI solutions.
