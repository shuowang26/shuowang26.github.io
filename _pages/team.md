I lead the AIM³ (AI for Multi-Modal Medicine) research group at Fudan University, located on the medical campus. Our interdisciplinary team tackles clinical challenges from affiliated hospitals, investigating the fundamental scientific principles underlying health and disease.

Our team members have diverse backgrounds in mathematics, mechanics, physics, computer science, electrical engineering, biomedical engineering, basic medicine, and clinical medicine.  We aim to accelerate medical discovery and translate our findings into improved patient care through AI.
